MIKO Child Safety Standards

Last updated: November 10, 2026

MIKO is an AI companion application developed by KIRO Enterprises. We are committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

MIKO has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. Any use of MIKO to promote, facilitate, request, create, distribute, or encourage child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or child exploitation is strictly prohibited.

2. Prohibited Activities

Users must not use MIKO to:

  • Create, request, upload, share, or distribute CSAM.
  • Sexually exploit, groom, threaten, or manipulate minors.
  • Encourage or facilitate sexual abuse of children.
  • Promote child trafficking or any form of child exploitation.
  • Use the application to arrange abusive or exploitative contact with minors.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users can report suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation, harmful content, or child safety concerns through our official support email:

kiro.support.official@gmail.com

Please include relevant details about the concern. Do not send, upload, or forward illegal sexual material involving children.

4. Review and Enforcement

Reports will be reviewed by the MIKO team. Where appropriate and legally permitted, we may restrict or terminate accounts, preserve relevant information, and report suspected illegal activity to the appropriate authorities.

5. Child Protection Commitment

KIRO Enterprises is committed to complying with applicable child protection laws and relevant platform safety requirements. We aim to maintain a safe environment and take child safety concerns seriously.

6. Contact

For child safety concerns or questions about this policy, contact:
