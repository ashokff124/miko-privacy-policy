MIKO AI

Privacy Policy

Developed and operated by KIRO Enterprises

1. Introduction

MIKO AI is an AI-powered companion application developed and operated by KIRO Enterprises. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use MIKO.

2. Information We Collect

MIKO currently uses username-and-password accounts. Email registration is not required. Depending on your use of the application, MIKO may process:

  • Username and account authentication information.
  • Chat messages and AI-generated responses.
  • Individual conversation history.
  • User-specific saved memories.
  • Text converted from voice input.

Passwords are used for account authentication. Never share your password with anyone.

3. Voice and Audio Privacy

  • MIKO does not store voice recordings.
  • Voice input is converted into text.
  • Converted text may be saved as part of your conversation history.

4. How We Use Information

Information is used to provide AI conversations, maintain chat history, support personalized memory features, authenticate accounts, and operate and protect MIKO.

5. Third-Party AI Services

MIKO currently uses the OpenAI API to process relevant message content and generate AI responses. Messages and conversation context needed to produce a response may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI handles API data under its applicable terms and policies. Its processing and retention practices may apply to information sent through the API.

MIKO is a product of KIRO Enterprises. OpenAI is an external AI service provider and is not the owner or developer of the MIKO application.

6. Data Storage and Security

MIKO maintains account information, conversations and saved memories through its backend and database infrastructure. KIRO Enterprises takes reasonable steps intended to protect information. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Account and Data Deletion

MIKO currently does not provide an in-app account deletion option. Users who wish to request account and associated data deletion can contact KIRO Enterprises at:

Email: kiro.support.official@gmail.com

Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable legal requirements and necessary verification. No specific deletion timeframe is promised. Please do not send your password in a deletion request.

8. Data Sharing

KIRO Enterprises does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to operate MIKO, including the AI service provider described in this policy, or disclosed where required by applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

Users should not provide children's personal information without appropriate parental or guardian authorization. If you believe a child has provided personal information to MIKO, contact us using the email below.

10. Changes to This Policy

KIRO Enterprises may update this Privacy Policy when MIKO's features, services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this webpage.
