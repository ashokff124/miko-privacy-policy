Privacy Policy

MIKO AI

Effective date: 9 November 2026Operated by KIRO Enterprises

1. Introduction

MIKO AI is an AI-powered companion application developed and operated by KIRO Enterprises. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use MIKO.

2. Information We Collect

MIKO currently uses username-and-password accounts. Email registration is not required. Depending on how you use the application, MIKO may process:

  • Your username and account authentication information.
  • Chat messages and AI-generated responses.
  • Conversation history associated with your account.
  • User-specific saved memories used to personalize conversations.
  • Text converted from voice input.

Passwords are used for account authentication. Do not share your password with anyone.

3. Voice and Audio

  • MIKO does not store voice recordings, according to its current design.
  • Voice input is converted into text for interaction with MIKO.
  • The resulting text may be saved as part of your conversation history.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide AI conversations, maintain account-specific chat history, support saved-memory features, authenticate users, and operate and protect the application.

5. Third-Party AI Processing

MIKO currently uses the OpenAI API to process relevant message content and generate AI responses. Messages and conversation context needed to produce a response may be transmitted to OpenAI for processing. OpenAI handles API data under its applicable terms and policies. Its processing and retention practices may apply to information sent through the API.

MIKO is a product of KIRO Enterprises. OpenAI is an external AI service provider and is not the owner or developer of the MIKO application.

6. Storage and Security

MIKO maintains account information, conversations and saved memories through its backend and database infrastructure. KIRO Enterprises takes reasonable steps intended to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Account and Data Deletion Requests

MIKO currently does not provide an in-app account deletion option. If you want to request deletion of your account and associated information, contact KIRO Enterprises at kiro.support.official@gmail.com. Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable legal requirements and any necessary verification.

This policy does not promise a specific deletion timeframe. Please do not include your password in a deletion request.

8. Sharing and Disclosure

KIRO Enterprises does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by service providers when needed to operate MIKO, including the AI service provider described above, or disclosed where required by applicable law.

9. Children's Privacy

MIKO is not designed to encourage children to submit personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to MIKO, please contact us using the details below so the concern can be reviewed.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when MIKO's features, service providers or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on the official MIKO Privacy Policy webpage. The effective date at the top indicates when this version takes effect.
